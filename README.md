# Value Desk – Personalized Membership Rewards Redemption Platform

**American Express Campus Challenge 2026 | Product Track**  
🏆 **All India Rank 2 – National Runner-Up**

## Overview

Value Desk is a personalized rewards redemption solution conceptualized as part of the American Express Campus Challenge 2026 Product Track. It aims to help American Express cardmembers maximize the value of their accumulated Membership Rewards points through intelligent, personalized redemption recommendations.

Many premium cardholders accumulate substantial reward points but struggle to identify the most valuable redemption opportunities. Value Desk addresses this gap by transforming a passive points balance into actionable, curated redemption strategies.

## Problem Statement

Despite accumulating significant Membership Rewards points, many cardmembers:

- Lack awareness of the different redemption possibilities available.
- Find it difficult to compare the monetary value of different redemption options.
- Miss opportunities to maximize point value through travel and partner transfers.
- Continue accumulating points without a clear redemption strategy.

This creates a gap between the rewards offered by American Express and the actual value customers realize from them.

## Our Solution

Value Desk acts as a personalized redemption assistant that evaluates customer requirements and recommends suitable ways to redeem Membership Rewards points.

### Key Features

**1. Personalized Redemption Recommendations**
- Customers describe their requirements in natural language.
- The system generates curated redemption paths based on their preferences and available options.

**2. Multi-Option Comparison**
- Presents multiple redemption alternatives.
- Compares the effective monetary value of each option.
- Helps customers understand the trade-offs between different redemption methods.

**3. Value Optimization Engine**
- Evaluates redemption options against a statement-credit baseline.
- Considers transfer partners, redemption ratios, and applicable bonus opportunities.
- Recommends holding points when immediate redemption does not provide sufficient value.

**4. Explainable Recommendations**
- Explains why a particular redemption option is recommended.
- Highlights the benefits, trade-offs, and potential value of each alternative.

**5. Customer-Centric Design**
- Focuses on making rewards more accessible and easier to understand.
- Encourages meaningful engagement with the Membership Rewards ecosystem.

## Product Strategy

Our approach was designed around a key customer segment: **Silent Accumulators**.

These are cardmembers who accumulate substantial reward points but do not actively redeem them for high-value rewards.

Instead of simply encouraging more spending, Value Desk focuses on improving the value customers derive from their existing rewards.

### Business Impact

The proposed solution aims to:

- Increase Membership Rewards redemption engagement.
- Improve perceived value of American Express products.
- Strengthen customer loyalty and retention.
- Encourage engagement with travel and partner redemption ecosystems.
- Improve long-term customer lifetime value.

## Recommendation Philosophy

Value Desk follows a value-first recommendation approach:

1. Understand the customer's redemption requirements.
2. Identify relevant redemption opportunities.
3. Calculate the effective value of each option.
4. Compare options against the baseline redemption value.
5. Recommend the most suitable option or suggest holding points.

The objective is not simply to maximize redemption activity, but to maximize meaningful customer value.
